# Concourse reference pass

Authorized scope: all 19 existing concourse concessions and both sides of the west main entry circulation. Match the supplied timber, charcoal stone, black columns, warm strips, coffee/snack/drinks and lounge reference. Existing stage, spectators, exterior and entrances remain intact.

Production target: the requested Blender desktop scene. One shared, physically modeled four-bay row and a compact two-bay variant beside the main entry. Budget: under 100,000 source triangles per row; under 2.1 million evaluated new triangles across 21 rows; two packed image atlases at their generated source resolution; at most 12 light instances per row. Existing floor and column geometry reused with isolated material slots.

Dimensions: row 15.2 m; compact row 7.6 m; bay pitch 3.8 m; counter worktop 1.115 m; fascia 2.855–3.305 m; ceiling underside 4.2 m; wood slat pitch 80 mm with 15 mm recess. Measured straight circulation clearance: main entry 3.181 m, ring minimum 2.547 m at existing columns. Gaps between neighboring rows and the main ramp/ticket gates are checked geometrically. These are model measurements, not surveyed building or code certification.

Final: 960,112 evaluated instanced triangles; shared source 42,184 triangles plus compact source 19,172; 11/8 local lights per full/compact row. Small equipment lights and toe lights have bounded influence and no shadow maps. Main and concourse-review Eevee shadow pool: 1024 MB to accommodate the existing arena plus added lighting.

Blender still visualization asset, PC only. No web/game export, target-device profiling or runtime LOD certification is part of this requested .blend edit. Texture atlas uses ordinary UV image nodes; glass is limited to equipment displays. Surface scale, geometry, packed assets, preservation checks and actual in-scene renders are the delivery checks.
